Sample data-series
  for invocation of "TestUtil.exe"
  via ImageJ-macro "TestUtil.ijm".

The names are mmm_pfx_xWWWWxHHHH.tif, where:
  = mmm - suggested "image process mode number" (e.g. "100");
  = pfx - identifies an object;
  = WWWWxHHHH - dimension (xWWWW when square);
  = suffix "std" denotes standard sample process (when available).

The files are:
  = 70n* - NDT X-ray radiography;
    the target is "thin ~horz wires inside iron pipe".
 
  = 71n* - NDT X-ray radiography;
    the target is "thin ~vert slits in general phantom".

  = 100_* - infrared images MS_KinectAzure;
    the target is ~"human observation".
