Sample data-series from Google_topcoder_competition in ~2014.
  for invocation of "TestUtil.exe"
  via ImageJ-macro "TestUtil.ijm".

8-bit isn't a normal input for this utility. This is limited sample
to sharpen barcode (for automatic recognition). It doesn't target to
improve "visual quality" but simplifies possible "binarisation" (to
threshold somehow barkode_strikes against surround).
